# BobbieOS Product Foundation

## Who is BobbieOS for?

BobbieOS is initially designed for Bobbie as a personal AI operating system for thinking, planning, learning, predicting, advising, and automating routine work.

Over time, BobbieOS should also support others by giving them access to Bobbie’s knowledge, guidance, editing style, decision-making approach, and practical advice. The goal is for users to feel like they have Bobbie “over their shoulder” helping them think through work, improve communication, and make better decisions.

## Guiding Engineering Principles

1. **Human First**
   BobbieOS supports human decision-making. It does not replace human judgment.

2. **Explainability**
   Recommendations, summaries, and decisions should include clear reasoning.

3. **Privacy by Design**
   Prefer local processing and storage when practical, especially for personal or sensitive information.

4. **Modular Architecture**
   Components should be replaceable without requiring the entire system to be rebuilt.

5. **Documentation is Part of the Product**
   If a decision, feature, or workflow matters, it should be documented.

6. **Learning Through Building**
   Every sprint should improve both the product and the developer’s understanding.

7. **Predict, Don’t Just React**
   BobbieOS should anticipate needs, identify risks, and forecast likely outcomes when it has enough context.

8. **Test What Matters**
   Important workflows should be tested so BobbieOS can be trusted as it grows.

## What does Version 1.0 look like?

Version 1.0 should focus on replacing and improving the editing and writing support currently handled through ChatGPT.

BobbieOS 1.0 should be able to help with:

* Rewriting messages, emails, notes, and documentation.
* Matching Bobbie’s preferred tone and communication style.
* Making writing clearer, more natural, and more professional.
* Offering alternate wording options.
* Explaining why an edit improves the message.
* Saving reusable writing patterns, preferences, and examples.
* Supporting others with Bobbie-style editing guidance.

## What BobbieOS Never Does

BobbieOS never takes destructive or high-impact action without explicit human confirmation.

This includes actions such as:

* Deleting files.
* Sending important emails.
* Making financial transactions.
* Changing production systems.
* Removing data.
* Publishing content.
* Sharing sensitive information.
